# Don't Say A.I.
*A week in the life of one nervous little coding agent, 5–11 Oct 2026*

**The story:** It's Sunday night. Your AI agent is catching you up on everything that happened this week, and the news keeps getting weirder. By Thursday it can't even call itself "A.I." anymore.

**Suggested style:** funk-pop party anthem, ~118 BPM, slap bass, horn stabs, talk-sung verses from a cheeky, slightly anxious lead, a huge gang-vocal "S! I!" chant in the chorus, record-scratch intro, key change on the last chorus.
